Design the routes of an area

Every house of a collection area or of a whole sub sector. Houses on one stretch of road become one street stop, a trip carries at most what the truck carries and ends at the tipping site, and trips fill vehicles to the length of a shift. Each input is prefilled from the weighbridge tickets and the fleet register, says where it came from, and can be changed. The design is stored as a proposed plan, never as the authoritative route.

Plan a few stops

Route optimisation for a handful of stops: registered bins, or the houses in the view. Stops are visited once each, vehicles carry a number of units. The plan is stored as a proposed scenario, never as the authoritative route.

Routing runs on a directed road network: one way streets, speed by road class, junction delays, and peak factors on main roads at 07 to 09, 13 to 15 and 17 to 19 (the traffic model; live traffic feeds come with the telematics phase). All equipment starts at Al Mullah Transit Station.

The model scores relative accumulation risk from eleven weighted factors, 0 to 100. It is not a modelled water depth and the band drawn is not an inundation extent. Moderate is 57 per cent of the grid at 100 mm, so drawing it shades most of the emirate. Drainage assets, wadi lines and flood history are still placeholders, so the score cannot yet separate the areas that drain.
